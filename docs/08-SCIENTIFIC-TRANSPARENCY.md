# Scientific Transparency

The Heliosonic Sonifier occupies a unique position at the intersection of
scientific data sonification and artistic musical expression. This document
states clearly which physical models are **verified**, which are
**theoretical**, and which are **artistic metaphors**.

> *"The system does not ask: 'What does a p-mode sound like?' It asks: 'How
> does a p-mode reorganize the internal state of the computational organism?'"*

---

## The epistemological framework

The system is grounded in a tripartite framework:

1. **Scientific data** (NOAA/SWPC) provides the *material foundation*
2. **Scientific concepts** (p-modes, solar cycle) serve as *structural inspiration*
3. **Scientific metaphors** (quantum mechanics, black-hole physics) function as
   *compositional constraints*

### Representation vs. resonance

| Representation | Resonance |
|----------------|-----------|
| Data is *translated* into sound | Data is *metabolized* by the system |
| One-to-one mapping | One-to-many transduction (1:N) |
| Sound is a "sign" of the data | Sound emerges from internal states |
| Composer controls the mapping | Composer curates the emergence |

## The computer-music tradition of scientific metaphor

The system participates in what Jean-Claude Risset termed the *"poetic
appropriation of scientific models."*

| Precedent | Scientific source | Musical outcome |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Iannis Xenakis (1971) | Stochastic physics | Probability-based composition |
| John Chowning | Acoustic FM theory | FM synthesis |
| Curtis Roads (2001) | Particle physics | Granular synthesis |
| Miller Puckette (1996) | Control theory | Real-time state machines |
| François Bayle | Acousmatic theory | Sound-object morphology |

## Responsible use of metaphor

The key to using scientific metaphor responsibly is to **operationalize** it —
translate the metaphor into specific computational algorithms whose behaviour
can be understood and analysed. For example, the mathematical form of the
quantum-tunneling equation is used as a generative rule for musical events,
not as a claim about physical reality.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Disclaimer.** This system uses physical concepts as artistic analogies. It
> is **not** a scientific simulation. The quantum models (tunneling,
> entanglement, superposition) are computational metaphors, not simulations of
> quantum physics. Black-hole analogies are artistic, not physical. This
> transparency is essential to the system's positioning as an artistic and
> educational tool, not a scientific instrument.
