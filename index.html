OVERVIEW

The Heliosonic Sonifier is a real-time artistic sonification instrument that transforms NOAA/SWPC space-weather data into adaptive MIDI music, bridging scientific data and musical expression through generative AI and computational creativity.

"How does solar wind become music? Not through representation, but through resonance. Not through translation, but through transduction."

AI SYSTEMS

StateAwareMarkovMelody

  • 3rd-order Markov · 8 states
  • ~80,000 transitions
  • LRU cache 200 entries · 95% hit
  • Genetic algorithms + Perlin + Rule 30

AdaptiveMemory (RL)

  • 1,000 episodic entries
  • Ensemble of 5 RBF models
  • Dynamic learning rate 2–60%
  • Auto-apply ≥ 0.65 confidence

GlitchEngine

  • 28 glitch types (quantum + solar)
  • Tunnelling P = exp(−2κL)
  • Throttle 10/s · max 3 simultaneous

AdaptiveStateManager

  • 8 states + 4 micro-states
  • Crossfade 200–500 ms (ADSR)
  • Adaptive hysteresis 0.08–0.20
  • ≤ 12 transitions / minute
R = 0.25(1−T) + 0.20E + 0.20C_opt + 0.20N + 0.15S
T=tension · E=energy · C_opt=chaotic optimality · N=note diversity · S=state stability
